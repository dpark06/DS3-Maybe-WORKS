    Feeding Guidelines

    Bodega cats keep stockrooms clear by chasing rats and mice, and are loved as neighborhood mascots and local celebrities. Feed adults 1 can per 3 lbs of body weight daily, in two or more meals. Even a working cat needs fresh water, always.

    1. 1
      Peel the tab. Lift the lid and listen for the pitter-patter of very excited paws.
    2. 2
